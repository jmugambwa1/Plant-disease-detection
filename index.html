FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which plant species does the system support?

14 plant species from the PlantVillage dataset — including Tomato, Apple, Corn, Grape, Potato, Pepper, Cherry, Peach, Strawberry, Raspberry, Squash, Soybean, Orange, and Blueberry — covering 38 disease classes in total.

How accurate is the model?

96.6% validation accuracy on the held-out PlantVillage test set. Performance is best when images are well-lit, in focus, and show a single leaf filling most of the frame.

What image format should I upload?

JPEG, PNG, or WebP up to 10 MB. Images are automatically resized to 224×224 px before inference. One clear leaf filling the frame gives the best results.

How does the AI model work?

Transfer learning on MobileNetV2 — pre-trained on ImageNet, with the final layers replaced and fine-tuned on 54,000+ PlantVillage images. The lightweight architecture enables real-time inference on CPU.

Does it detect multiple diseases at once?

The system returns the top 3 most probable disease classifications with confidence scores. For complex co-infections or ambiguous symptoms, consult an agricultural expert.
